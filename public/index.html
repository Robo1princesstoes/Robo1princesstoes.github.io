Welcome to Cats in Knead

Cats In Knead (CIK) is a small, volunteer-based cat rescue group in Butler County PA. We are a 501 (c) 3 non-profit organization, striving to reduce the number of stray cats & kittens in our local communities. We do this by providing in-home foster care & adoption, hosting spay/neuter/vaccine clinics and assisting/coordinating TNR projects. Our fundraising supports not only our cats and kittens in foster care, but also medical care of owned cats when a verified financial need exists.

Volunteer With Us

Join our dedicated team of volunteers making a difference in the lives of cats in Butler County! Whether you have a few hours a week or a few hours a month, we have opportunities that fit your schedule. The volunteers at cats in knead spend many hours working toward fundraising, scheduling clinics, volunteering at events and fostering. WE appreciate everyone’s time and respect their personal privacy at times when they are not volunteering. For this reason, we do not list personal phone numbers of our group members. If a phone number is listed to contact for a specific event, please only call that number if in relation to the event.
Reaching out to us via email, or via facebook messenger, is the best way to reach us at this time.

Adoption Questions

How long will it take to complete the application?

We expect it will take between 30 and 45 minutes to complete and then submit our application via Google Forms.

Why do you ask so many personal questions?

The questions we ask are directly related to helping us understand how you will care for your new cat/kitten. Since our ultimate goal is to place our fosters in a safe and loving forever home, we need to be extremely sure we are making the best match possible!!

Do you check references?

YES, YES AND YES!! You will provide us with 2 NON-FAMILY personal references as well as a vet reference. We contact them by phone and ask a few questions about your character and what would make you a great pet parent. When speaking with the vet, we ask about your previous/current pets and general care you have provided based on vet recommendations.

What happens if this is my first pet and I don't have a vet yet?

If you do not yet have a veterinarian, we ask that you call a few and determine at least 2 that you plan to take your cat/kitten to after adoption. We can give recommendations for vets in your area~just ask us!!

When can I meet the Cat/Kitten I'm interested in?

We only allow meet and greets to occur AFTER your adoption application is approved. We do not want to waste anyone’s time (our foster or yours) if your application doesn’t meet our standards.

How do you decide which applications to approve or deny?

Your responses to the application questions are our basis for general approval/denial. We reserve the right to contact you for clarification of any response. Further, we reserve the right to conduct a home visit (virtual or in person) to insure your space is adequate for the care of your new cat/kitten. Cats In Knead does not discriminate on the basis of race, color, religion (creed), gender, gender expression, age, national origin (ancestry), disability, marital status, sexual orientation, veteran status, or any other characteristic protected under applicable federal, state, or local laws. This commitment extends to all aspects of our organization, including adoptions, volunteer opportunities, program participation, and service provision.

When can I take my new Cat/Kitten home?

Because applications are approved before meet and greets, we typically allow adopters to take their cat/kitten home directly from the meet and greet, if they feel that the match is perfect. If not, because the application is approved, the adopter may choose another cat they have a connection with/are interested in at the meet and greet, or another cat CIK has in foster care.

What happens after adoption?

We will let you know if your cat is in need of any medical updates at the time of adoption. We do ask that you make an appointment with your vet to establish your new cat/kitten, in the event an emergency arises. This should occur within 2 weeks of adoption. You are not required to notify us of this visit, but we do reserve the right to check in at any time to insure your cat is being well cared for. We LOVE updates and would like to feature your adoption story on our social media, so please, send us pictures and updates as often as you like!!

Foster Program Questions

What does fostering involve?

Fostering means providing a temporary home for cats until they find their forever families. We provide all supplies (food, litter, toys), medical care, and support. You provide love, socialization, and a safe environment. Foster periods typically range from 2 weeks to several months.

Do I need to have experience with cats to foster?

No! We welcome first-time foster parents and provide comprehensive training and ongoing support. We'll match you with cats that fit your experience level and comfort zone.

Can I foster if I have other pets?

Yes! Many of our foster families have other pets. We'll work with you to find foster cats that will be compatible with your existing pets and provide guidance on successful introductions.

General Questions

Where are you located?

Cats in Knead operates throughout Butler County, PA. Our cats are in foster homes throughout the county. We hold adoption events at various locations - check our Events Calendar for upcoming meet-and-greet opportunities! For more information, please contact us, or check out the contact page.

How can I donate?

We accept monetary donations through our website, checks by mail, and items from our Amazon wishlist. Donations are tax-deductible as we are a 501(c)(3) non-profit organization. Visit our Contact & Donate page for more information.

Can I sponsor a Cat/Kitten?

Absolutely. We are always looking for sponsors to help us cover the costs of medical care, food, and supplies for our cats. If you would like to sponsor a cat, please contact us via email at catsinknead2024@gmail.com.
